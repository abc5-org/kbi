2 · Decisions Recorded

The following decisions frame the proposed structure. Everything not covered by a decision below remains a proposal open to amendment.

Decisions

Recorded 30 October 2026

  1. ERTW Director — TO BE DECIDED
    The appointment of the ERTW Director will be decided soon. The office is vacant in the meantime, and the structure below is written so that it holds regardless of who is appointed: the Director's authority comes from the office, not the person.
  2. Contracts are signed by the MTÜ.
    All ERTW contracts are signed by the MTÜ (Königsberger Brückeninstitut MTÜ), KBI's Estonian parent entity — regardless of which office negotiated them or which approval level authorised the spend. No member of this committee signs contracts in their own right. Payment and transaction arrangements are TO BE DECIDED at a later date.
  3. Clarification — whose votes are in question.
    The open voting question concerns this Organizing Committee only: whether Oxford student societies would ever hold voting seats on it. It does not concern the KBI Expert Councils — separate subject-matter-expert networks, which may nominate advisers or adjudicators but hold no vote here — nor KBI's Governance Council or Board of Directors. Current position: the societies sit in the Advisory Circle, non-voting.
  4. Interim spending authority (GBP).
    Until KBI's general approval brackets are settled, the following interim limits apply. Approval authorises the spend; it does not sign anything — contracts are executed only by the MTÜ under Decision 2.

3 · Roles & Responsibilities

One card per body and role. Spending figures refer to the interim authority in Decision 4; no card confers contract-signing power, which rests with the MTÜ alone.

The Steering Committee (as a body)

Decision layer · 7 voting seats, held by office

Purpose

The committee's decision-making body. It sets direction, holds the budget envelope, and reserves to itself — with Board ratification where required — the decisions that carry legal, financial, safeguarding or reputational weight.

Responsibilities

  • Approve the event budget envelope, the programme shape, and the venue and accommodation strategy.
  • Decide reserved matters: major contracts, the safeguarding policy, sponsor exclusivity, and anything touching adjudication outcomes or KBI's name and intellectual property.
  • Run the four gate reviews with the Advisory Circle (December 2026, March 2027, September 2027, October 2027).
  • Appoint workstream Leads and Deputies, on the ERTW Director's recommendation.
  • Approve spending up to £1,000 per commitment; escalate anything above to the Board / Governance Council.
  • Keep a written decision log, updated within 24 hours of every decision.

Authority & limits

Decides by vote — one person, one vote; quorum is five of seven, and must include the Finance, Legal & Contracts seat for any money or contract decision. The committee authorises; it does not sign. All contracts are executed by the MTÜ.

Reports to: KBI Board of Directors / Governance Council, on reserved matters. Cadence: fortnightly until December 2026; monthly January–June 2027; fortnightly July–September 2027; weekly October–November 2027; daily during the event week.

Chair

Steering seat 1 of 7

Purpose

Leads the Steering Committee and is its point of final accountability to the Board — deliberately distinct from the day-to-day operator.

Responsibilities

  • Chair Steering Committee meetings; set agendas with the ERTW Director.
  • Own reserved matters and bring them to the Board / Governance Council for ratification.
  • Act as the final escalation point for deadlocks and cross-workstream disputes.
  • Serve as the endpoint of the safeguarding escalation bypass.
  • Represent the committee to the Board and, where required, externally.

Authority & limits

Casting responsibility for the committee's process and escalations; no unilateral spending authority beyond the Steering Committee's collective £1,000 limit, and no contract signature (MTÜ only).

Reports to: Board of Directors / Governance Council. Cadence: chairs every Steering meeting; available for safeguarding escalation at all times.

ERTW Director / Vice-Chair

Steering seat 2 of 7 · Vacant — appointment TO BE DECIDED

Purpose

The single accountable day-to-day operator of ERTW'27 Oxford. Everything in the delivery layer runs through this office; if a task has no owner, it belongs to the Director until assigned.

Responsibilities

  • Own the master plan, milestones and critical path from appointment to closing ceremony.
  • Coordinate the eight workstreams; run the weekly delivery meeting.
  • Maintain the decision log and ensure every Steering decision is recorded within 24 hours.
  • Recommend workstream Leads and Deputies to the Steering Committee.
  • Escalate risks, slippage and safeguarding matters promptly — noting that safeguarding may also bypass this office entirely.
  • Approve spending up to £500 per commitment.

Authority & limits

Approves up to £500; cannot sign contracts (the MTÜ signs); cannot overrule the adjudication firewall or a safeguarding escalation.

Reports to: Steering Committee, through the Chair. Cadence: weekly delivery meeting; reports to every Steering meeting.

Strategy & Risk

Steering seat · K1 office

Purpose

Keeps the project pointed at its milestones and honest about its risks, and keeps ETO West aligned with ETO East.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the milestone plan and track it against the four gates.
  • Own the risk register: identify, score, assign and review risks at every Steering meeting.
  • Coordinate East/West alignment with the ETO East counterpart in the Advisory Circle.
  • Prepare and run the gate reviews (outreach lock-down, programme lock, readiness, final ops).
  • Flag strategic drift — scope, dates, or commitments creeping beyond the approved envelope.

Authority & limits

Advisory and monitoring authority within the Steering Committee's vote; no separate spending authority beyond the committee's collective limit.

Reports to: Steering Committee. Cadence: reports at every Steering meeting; gate reviews December 2026, March, September and October 2027.

Finance, Legal & Contracts

Steering seat · K2 office

Purpose

Guardian of the budget and of KBI's legal position. Every pound and every signature passes this office's scrutiny — though the signature itself is the MTÜ's.

Responsibilities

  • Control the budget: track commitments against the envelope and the interim spending limits.
  • Run procurement proportionately — quotes, comparisons and records for significant purchases.
  • Arrange insurance appropriate to a residential international student event in the UK.
  • Draft, review and route every contract to the MTÜ for signature; keep the contract register.
  • Maintain the conflict-of-interest register; ensure conflicts are declared and minuted.
  • Advise the committee on the contracting-entity question for UK activity as arrangements are settled.

Authority & limits

Required member of the quorum for any money or contract decision. May block a commitment that lacks budget cover or a route to MTÜ signature; cannot sign contracts personally.

Reports to: Steering Committee; liaises with the MTÜ on all signatures. Cadence: budget report at every Steering meeting.

Communications & Community

Steering seat · K3 office

Purpose

Owns how the event speaks — to participants, hosts, partners and the wider KBI community — so that everyone hears one consistent account.

Responsibilities

  • Own participant communications: joining instructions, schedule changes, on-the-week announcements.
  • Set the announcement calendar and clear external statements with the Chair where they carry commitments.
  • Steward community and society relations, jointly with the K6 seat where host institutions are concerned.
  • Oversee the Communications & Content workstream, including consent for photography.
  • Handle press and public enquiries, escalating sensitive matters to the Chair.

Authority & limits

May publish operational information within agreed lines; may not announce commitments, prices or partnerships that the Steering Committee has not approved.

Reports to: Steering Committee. Cadence: reports at every Steering meeting; daily comms rhythm during the event week.

Programme & Adjudication Integrity

Steering seat · K5 office, supported by the K4 office

Purpose

Protects the credibility of ETO West 2027: its rules, its judging, its results and its appeals. This seat is the committee-side anchor of the adjudication firewall.

Responsibilities

  • Own the ETO competition rules, judging criteria and scoring method for the Oxford week.
  • Constitute judging panels — drawing on Expert Council nominees and host experts — free of conflicts.
  • Guarantee results integrity: secure handling of scores, verification before announcement.
  • Run the appeals process, with published grounds, deadlines and decision-makers.
  • Shape the technical programme and site-visit content, with support from the K4 office on innovation and technology content.

Authority & limits

Independent of partnerships and sponsorship: no sponsor, host or partner may influence judging, results or appeals, and this seat must refuse and minute any such approach. No spending authority beyond the committee's collective limit.

Reports to: Steering Committee; adjudication decisions are final within the published appeals process. Cadence: reports at every Steering meeting; intensive during programme lock (March 2027) and the event week.

Partnerships & Hosts

Steering seat · K6 office

Purpose

Builds the relationships the week depends on — host institutions, the venue, partner events and sponsors — inside the authority the committee has actually granted.

Responsibilities

  • Lead outreach to host institutions, site-visit hosts and prospective partner events.
  • Negotiate venue and partner arrangements for Steering approval and MTÜ signature.
  • Oversee the Partnerships & Sponsorship workstream and the sponsor pipeline.
  • Coordinate society relations jointly with the K3 seat.
  • Ensure no partner is promised exclusivity, visibility or access beyond the approved envelope.

Authority & limits

May negotiate; may not commit KBI beyond the Steering Committee's authority, sign anything, or have any contact with adjudication on the merits of any team or entry.

Reports to: Steering Committee. Cadence: outreach status at every Steering meeting; priority to the December 2026 outreach lock-down.

The Delivery Team (as a body)

Delivery layer · 8 workstreams under the ERTW Director

Purpose

Executes what the Steering Committee decides. Each workstream is run by a Lead with a named Deputy, so that no function depends on a single person during a residential week with dozens of travelling students.

Responsibilities

  • Deliver the workstream plans and budgets approved by the Steering Committee.
  • Report progress, risks and spend to the ERTW Director weekly.
  • Approve workstream spending up to £100 per commitment, within agreed budget lines.
  • Escalate early: safeguarding immediately, everything else through the Director.

Authority & limits

Operational authority within approved plans only. No workstream may sign contracts, vary the programme's adjudication arrangements, or commit KBI externally.

Reports to: ERTW Director. Cadence: weekly delivery meeting, chaired by the Director; daily during the event week.

Workstream 1 · Programme & Site Visits

Delivery · Lead + Deputy

Purpose

Turns the week's outline into a timed, briefed, walkable programme of sessions and site visits.

Responsibilities

  • Build and maintain the day-by-day visit schedule for the week.
  • Brief every host: group size, timing, session aims, accessibility needs.
  • Confirm bookings, capacities and group-size limits with each venue and host.
  • Plan walking routes and timings between sites; hold wet-weather fallbacks.
  • Feed programme changes to Communications & Content for participant updates.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100 per commitment within budget lines. Programme content with adjudication implications goes through the K5 seat.

Reports to: ERTW Director. Cadence: weekly delivery meeting.

Workstream 2 · ETO Competition Operations

Delivery · Lead + Deputy · inside the adjudication firewall

Purpose

Runs the mechanics of ETO West 2027 — entries, exhibition, pitching and judges' operations — to the rules set by the K5 seat.

Responsibilities

  • Manage team entries, eligibility checks and the competition timetable.
  • Run Exhibition Day and Pitch Day logistics: rooms, order of play, equipment, timing.
  • Operate judges' logistics: panel scheduling, score collection, secure handling of results.
  • Administer the appeals timetable as directed by the K5 seat.
  • Keep competition operations strictly separate from sponsors and hosts.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100. No discretion over rules, judging or results — those belong to the K5 seat; no sponsor or host contact on the merits of any entry.

Reports to: ERTW Director; on rules and integrity, to the Programme & Adjudication Integrity seat. Cadence: weekly; daily during the event week.

Workstream 3 · Oxford Logistics

Delivery · Lead + Deputy

Purpose

The physical week: venue, beds, food and movement, arranged so participants can simply be where they need to be.

Responsibilities

  • Run venue operations at the ETO venue (Oxford Edge) and any satellite rooms.
  • Book and manage accommodation for participants and chaperones.
  • Arrange catering, including dietary requirements collected in advance.
  • Plan local transport, defaulting to walking; arrange alternatives where distance or accessibility requires.
  • Ensure accessibility needs are identified early and met in venue and route choices.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100 per commitment; bookings above that follow the approval ladder, and all contracts go to the MTÜ for signature.

Reports to: ERTW Director. Cadence: weekly; on-site throughout the event week.

Workstream 4 · Participant Care & Safeguarding

Delivery · Lead + Deputy · includes the Safeguarding Lead function

Purpose

Looks after the people: students and chaperones travelling internationally, many of them young, for a residential week. This workstream houses the event's named Safeguarding Lead and deputy.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the safeguarding policy for Steering approval and Board ratification; brief all staff and volunteers on it.
  • Set and monitor chaperone ratios and supervision arrangements.
  • Collect and safeguard medical, dietary, emergency-contact and travel information, on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Publish and enforce codes of conduct for participants, chaperones and staff.
  • Run incident response: record, act, escalate — with a direct line to the Chair that bypasses the ERTW Director.

Authority & limits

The Safeguarding Lead may halt any activity on welfare grounds, pending Steering review, and may escalate directly to the Chair at any time. Spending to £100, with welfare-critical spend escalated immediately rather than delayed.

Reports to: ERTW Director for operations; Steering Committee Chair, directly and without intermediary, for safeguarding escalation. Cadence: weekly; continuously on duty during the event week.

Workstream 5 · Partnerships & Sponsorship

Delivery · Lead + Deputy · outside the adjudication firewall

Purpose

Works the sponsor and partner pipeline day to day, under the direction of the K6 Steering seat.

Responsibilities

  • Research, approach and track prospective sponsors and partners.
  • Prepare sponsorship materials strictly within the approved offer and envelope.
  • Service confirmed sponsors: agreed visibility, hospitality and reporting.
  • Log every commitment offered or made, for the K2 seat's contract register.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100. May not promise exclusivity or benefits beyond the approved envelope, and may have no contact with adjudication on the merits of any team, entry or result.

Reports to: ERTW Director; functionally to the Partnerships & Hosts (K6) seat. Cadence: weekly delivery meeting.

Workstream 6 · Finance & Administration

Delivery · Lead + Deputy

Purpose

The paper trail: payments processed, receipts kept, and the budget position always knowable at a glance.

Responsibilities

  • Process payments and reimbursements once properly approved and, where a contract exists, signed by the MTÜ.
  • Track actuals against budget lines; flag variances to the K2 seat early.
  • Keep receipts, invoices and approval records complete and retrievable.
  • Administer registrations, lists and routine correspondence for the committee.

Authority & limits

Executes; does not approve its own spending beyond the £100 workstream limit, and does not vary approval decisions. Transaction arrangements themselves are TO BE DECIDED.

Reports to: ERTW Director; functionally to the Finance, Legal & Contracts (K2) seat. Cadence: weekly; budget pack for every Steering meeting.

Workstream 7 · Communications & Content

Delivery · Lead + Deputy

Purpose

Produces the event's public and participant-facing material, under the K3 seat's direction.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain the event pages and artefacts: itinerary, committee and information pages.
  • Produce announcements, schedules and participant information packs.
  • Manage photography and recording consent for all participants, with opt-outs honoured in practice.
  • Support press activity and capture the week's record for KBI's archive.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100. Publishes operational information within the K3 seat's agreed lines; announcements carrying commitments need Steering approval first.

Reports to: ERTW Director; functionally to the Communications & Community (K3) seat. Cadence: weekly; daily publishing rhythm during the event week.

Workstream 8 · Society & Volunteer Liaison

Delivery · Lead + Deputy

Purpose

The human bridge to Oxford's student societies and to the volunteers who will help run the week.

Responsibilities

  • Liaise with OxCyS, OxAI and the Oxford Blockchain Society while their involvement remains unconfirmed, keeping expectations accurate on both sides.
  • Channel society input into the Advisory Circle and the four gate reviews.
  • Recruit, brief and roster volunteers for the event week.
  • Ensure volunteers are covered by the safeguarding policy and codes of conduct.

Authority & limits

Spending to £100. May not offer societies voting seats, co-organizer status or programme commitments — those are Steering decisions, and the voting question remains open (Decision 3).

Reports to: ERTW Director. Cadence: weekly delivery meeting.

The Advisory Circle

Advisory layer · non-voting

Purpose

Brings Oxford knowledge, subject expertise and East/West perspective into the committee's decisions — without holding any of its authority. Membership: Oxford host institutions and venue contacts; Oxford student societies (OxCyS, OxAI, Oxford Blockchain Society — none confirmed as co-organizer); nominees of the KBI Expert Councils; and the ETO East counterpart.

Responsibilities

  • Advise at the four gates: outreach lock-down (December 2026), programme lock (March 2027), readiness review (September 2027), and final ops review (October 2027).
  • Give local and specialist advice between gates when the Steering Committee asks for it.
  • Expert Council nominees may serve as adjudicators under the K5 seat's rules.
  • Declare interests openly, especially where advice touches their own institutions.

Authority & limits

None, formally: no votes, no spending authority, and no authority to commit KBI in any way. Advice is received, minuted, and either adopted or set aside by the Steering Committee with reasons recorded.

Reports to: the Steering Committee, as a body. Cadence: the four gate reviews; otherwise by invitation.

4 · Working Rules

How the committee runs itself between now and November 2027.

  • Quorum. Five of the seven Steering seats, and for any decision involving money or contracts the Finance, Legal & Contracts (K2) seat must be among them.
  • One person, one vote. Each seat carries one vote regardless of how many functions its holder also performs elsewhere in the structure. Advisory Circle members do not vote.
  • Decision log. Every decision is recorded in a written decision log within 24 hours, with the decision, the mover, the vote and any dissent noted.
  • Conflicts of interest. Conflicts are declared at the start of the relevant item, recorded in the K2 seat's register, and minuted; a conflicted member takes no part in that decision. Approaches from sponsors or hosts touching adjudication are declared to the K5 seat and minuted, whoever receives them.
  • Spending. The interim ladder in Decision 4 applies — £100 / £500 / £1,000 / Board — and every contract, at every level, is signed by the MTÜ and no one else.
  • Safeguarding escalation. The Safeguarding Lead's direct line to the Chair bypasses the ERTW Director and may be used at any time, without notice and without needing to justify the bypass itself.
  • Vacancies. The ERTW Director office is vacant pending appointment (Decision 1). Until then, Steering decisions are carried by the Chair, and no delivery-layer commitment above £100 should be made except by Steering decision.
